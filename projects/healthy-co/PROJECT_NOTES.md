# Healthy Co — Patient Experience & Wait-Time Analysis

**Business question:** Where are patient waiting-time and wait-cost issues concentrated, and what operational practices could improve patient experience?

**Key analytical findings from the supplied model:**
- Average wait for doctor: Dr. Balla 29.63 min; Dr. Campbell 21.85; Dr. Campos 21.41; Dr. Jankowski 21.80; Dr. Yung 21.65.
- Average doctor wait cost per visit: Dr. Balla $1,481.64; Dr. Campbell $1,092.35; Dr. Campos $1,070.68; Dr. Jankowski $1,090.05; Dr. Yung $1,082.44.
- Average vital-wait cost by nurse is highest for Knight F at $166.21 per wait.
- Peak average total waiting times occur around 9, 12, and 19 GMT, at 47.6, 48.3, and 46.4 minutes respectively.
